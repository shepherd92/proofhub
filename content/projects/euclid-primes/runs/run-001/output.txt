Candidate natural-language proof

Assume for contradiction that there are only finitely many primes p_1, ..., p_n. Consider

    M = p_1 * ... * p_n + 1.

Every integer greater than one has a prime divisor, so let q divide M. If q were one of p_1, ..., p_n, then q would divide both their product and M, hence q would divide 1. This is impossible. Therefore q is a prime not in the supposedly complete list, a contradiction. There are infinitely many primes.
